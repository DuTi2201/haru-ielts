# Destination B1 — IELTS-style speaking sets for the 14 vocabulary units, written for this course.
# P1: question ; question ; question | nghĩa | model answer to the first question (2–3 sentences)
# P2: cue card | bullet; bullet; bullet | nghĩa | model answer (story: what → describe → why → what happened to me)
# P3: question ; question | nghĩa | model answer to the first question (opinion → reason → example)
#U 3
P1: Do you play any sports? ; What kind of music do you listen to? ; Did you play board games when you were a child? | Bạn có chơi môn thể thao nào không? Bạn nghe loại nhạc gì? Hồi nhỏ bạn có chơi cờ bàn không? | Yes, I do. I play badminton twice a week with two colleagues. I am not very good at it, but I do it for fun.
P2: Describe a game or competition you took part in. | when it was; who you played with or against; what happened | Kể về một trò chơi hoặc cuộc thi bạn đã tham gia: khi nào, chơi cùng ai hoặc đấu với ai, chuyện gì xảy ra, và bạn cảm thấy thế nào. | Last summer I took part in a football competition at my company. There were six teams, and I was the captain of mine. We trained twice a week for a month. In the first match our opponents scored two goals, but we did not give up, and we beat them in the end. We lost the final, but I had a lot of fun, and I got to know my colleagues much better.
P3: Why do some people prefer team sports to individual sports? ; Are video games good or bad for children? | Vì sao một số người thích thể thao đồng đội hơn thể thao cá nhân? Trò chơi điện tử tốt hay xấu cho trẻ em? | I think many people prefer team sports because they are more social. When you are a member of a team, the other players support you, so you train harder. For example, my brother gave up running after a month, but he has played for the same football club for years.
#U 6
P1: What subject did you like most at school? ; Do you prefer to study alone or with other people? ; How do you revise before an exam? | Ở trường bạn thích môn nào nhất? Bạn thích học một mình hay học cùng người khác? Bạn ôn bài thế nào trước kỳ thi? | I liked maths most. I was not the most talented student, but I enjoyed working out the answers. My teacher was very clever and explained everything clearly.
P2: Describe a skill you learned outside school. | what the skill is; who taught you; how you practised | Kể về một kỹ năng bạn học được ngoài nhà trường: kỹ năng gì, ai dạy, bạn luyện thế nào, và vì sao nó có ích. | A skill I learned outside school is cooking. My grandmother taught me when I was fifteen. At first I had to follow her instructions step by step, and I failed many times. I practised every weekend and slowly made progress. Now I can cook most family meals without a recipe. It is a useful skill because I save money, and I feel that I have achieved something every time my family enjoys a meal.
P3: Is it better to learn from a teacher or from the internet? ; Why do some adults find it hard to learn new things? | Học với giáo viên hay học qua mạng thì tốt hơn? Vì sao một số người lớn thấy khó học cái mới? | In my opinion, a teacher is better at the beginning. A good teacher can correct your mistakes at once, but a video cannot. For instance, I studied English pronunciation online for a year and made little progress, and then a teacher fixed two of my problems in one lesson.
#U 9
P1: How do you usually travel to work or school? ; Do you like travelling by plane? ; Where did you go on your last holiday? | Bạn thường đi làm hoặc đi học bằng gì? Bạn có thích đi máy bay không? Kỳ nghỉ gần nhất bạn đi đâu? | I usually go by motorbike. Public transport is not very convenient where I live, and the traffic is terrible. The journey takes about thirty minutes.
P2: Describe a place you would like to visit. | where it is; how you would travel there; what you would do there | Kể về một nơi bạn muốn đến: ở đâu, bạn sẽ đi bằng gì, sẽ làm gì ở đó, và vì sao bạn muốn đi. | A place I would really like to visit is Hoi An. It is an old town on the coast of central Vietnam. I would catch a flight to Da Nang and then take a coach, because the town has no airport. I would book accommodation near the river, walk around the old streets and buy a few souvenirs. I want to go because my parents went there on their first trip together, and they still talk about it.
P3: Why do many people prefer their own vehicle to public transport? ; Does travelling abroad change people? | Vì sao nhiều người thích xe riêng hơn giao thông công cộng? Đi nước ngoài có làm con người thay đổi không? | I think the main reason is convenience. With your own vehicle you can leave when you want, and you do not have to wait at a crowded platform. In my city, for example, buses are often delayed, so most people choose a motorbike even for short trips.
#U 12
P1: Do you live in a house or a flat? ; How often do you see your relations? ; Do you know your neighbours well? | Bạn sống ở nhà riêng hay căn hộ? Bạn có hay gặp họ hàng không? Bạn có biết rõ hàng xóm không? | I live in a small flat near the city centre. I rent it with my sister. It is not large, but the neighbourhood is quiet and friendly.
P2: Describe a time when a friend helped you. | who the friend is; what the problem was; what he or she did | Kể về một lần bạn bè giúp bạn: người đó là ai, bạn gặp chuyện gì, người đó đã làm gì, và bạn cảm thấy thế nào. | I would like to talk about my friend Hoa. We have been close since university. Two years ago I had to move out of my flat in one week, and I did not know anyone who could help. Hoa let me stay in her flat for a month and even helped me decorate my new room. She is generous and patient, and she never made me feel like a guest. I am still very grateful, and I trust her completely.
P3: Is it better to have a few close friends or many friends? ; Why do many young people today prefer to live alone? | Có vài người bạn thân hay có nhiều bạn thì tốt hơn? Vì sao nhiều người trẻ ngày nay thích sống một mình? | I believe a few close friends are better. You can trust them with private things, and they are loyal when you have a problem. I have hundreds of contacts online, for example, but when I was ill last year only two friends came to look after me.
#U 15
P1: Do you enjoy shopping? ; Do you usually pay in cash or by card? ; What was the last thing you bought for yourself? | Bạn có thích mua sắm không? Bạn thường trả tiền mặt hay trả bằng thẻ? Món gần nhất bạn mua cho mình là gì? | Not really. I only go shopping when I need something, and I try not to waste money. I prefer small shops, because the big ones are too crowded.
P2: Describe something expensive that you bought. | what it was; how you paid for it; whether it was worth the money | Kể về một món đồ đắt tiền bạn đã mua: món gì, bạn trả tiền thế nào, có đáng tiền không, và vì sao bạn mua nó. | The most expensive thing I have ever bought is my laptop. I purchased it three years ago, when my old one broke down. I could not afford it at once, so I saved up for four months and then paid in cash. I compared prices in several shops and found a real bargain in a sale. It cost a fortune for me at that time, but I use it every day for work and study, so I think it was worth the money.
P3: Why do people buy things that they do not need? ; Should children learn about money at school? | Vì sao người ta mua những thứ không cần? Trẻ em có nên học về tiền bạc ở trường không? | I think advertisements are the main cause. They make a product look like a bargain, so customers feel that they are saving money when they are really spending it. My sister, for example, bought three pairs of shoes in a sale last month and has not worn any of them.
#U 18
P1: What gadget do you use most often? ; Do you like learning to use new technology? ; How often do you use a laptop? | Bạn dùng thiết bị nào nhiều nhất? Bạn có thích học dùng công nghệ mới không? Bạn có hay dùng máy tính xách tay không? | I use my phone most. It is the first thing I look at in the morning. I use it to read, to pay bills and to find out what my friends are doing.
P2: Describe an invention that you think is important. | what it is; how people use it; how it has changed daily life | Kể về một phát minh bạn cho là quan trọng: nó là gì, người ta dùng thế nào, nó thay đổi đời sống ra sao, và vì sao bạn thấy nó quan trọng. | An invention that I think is very important is the washing machine. It is automatic and not complicated to operate: you put the clothes in, choose a program and turn it on. Before it was invented, people washed everything by hand, which took hours. My mother says that it gave her time to study in the evenings. For me it is important because it had a huge effect on ordinary families, especially on women.
P3: Has technology made our lives easier? ; Should young children use computers at school? | Công nghệ có làm cuộc sống dễ dàng hơn không? Trẻ nhỏ có nên dùng máy tính ở trường không? | Yes, I think it has, especially for communication and information. Jobs that involved hours of work can now be done in minutes. For example, I used to go to the bank to pay bills, and now I do it on my phone in a few seconds. However, when the equipment breaks down, we realise how much we depend on it.
#U 21
P1: How do you usually keep in touch with your friends? ; Do you watch TV every day? ; Do you prefer to phone people or to send messages? | Bạn thường giữ liên lạc với bạn bè bằng cách nào? Bạn có xem tivi hằng ngày không? Bạn thích gọi điện hay nhắn tin hơn? | Mostly online. I send messages during the day, and I ring my closest friends at the weekend. I hardly ever write emails to friends, because email feels too formal.
P2: Describe a website or app that you often use. | what it is; what you use it for; how often you use it | Kể về một trang web hoặc ứng dụng bạn hay dùng: nó là gì, bạn dùng để làm gì, dùng thường xuyên thế nào, và vì sao bạn thích. | An app that I use every day is a news app. It collects reports from several newspapers and broadcasts, so I do not have to visit each website. I usually open it on the bus, click on two or three links and read them carefully. I like it because the language is clear and I can listen to the reports when my eyes are tired. It is also useful for my English, because some of the reports are from foreign media.
P3: Do you think people can trust the news on the internet? ; How has the internet changed the way people communicate? | Bạn nghĩ người ta có thể tin tin tức trên mạng không? Internet đã thay đổi cách con người giao tiếp thế nào? | Not always, in my view. Anyone can publish a report online, and many people share it before they check it. Last year, for instance, a false announcement about a school closing reached thousands of parents in my city in one evening. So I think we should compare two or three websites before we believe a story.
#U 24
P1: What is your daily routine like? ; Is the area where you live safe? ; Do you have any habits that you want to change? | Lịch sinh hoạt hằng ngày của bạn thế nào? Khu bạn sống có an toàn không? Bạn có thói quen nào muốn thay đổi không? | My routine is quite typical. I get up at six, work until five and cook dinner in the evening. My schedule only changes at the weekend.
P2: Describe a tradition in your country that you like. | what the tradition is; when it happens; what people do | Kể về một truyền thống ở nước bạn mà bạn thích: truyền thống gì, diễn ra khi nào, mọi người làm gì, và vì sao bạn thích. | A tradition that I love is visiting relatives at Tet, the lunar new year. It happens in the first three days of the year. Families clean their houses, cook special food and visit older relations to wish them good health. Children are given lucky money. I like it because it is a social time: people forgive old arguments, and the whole community feels closer. Even those who work far away are responsible for coming home.
P3: Why do some young people commit crimes? ; Should people do more for their community? | Vì sao một số người trẻ phạm tội? Mọi người có nên làm nhiều hơn cho cộng đồng không? | I think there are two main reasons: poverty and bad company. A young person with no job and no support may steal because it looks easy. In my hometown, for example, crime fell after a youth club opened, because teenagers finally had a place to go in the evenings.
#U 27
P1: Do you work or are you a student? ; What do you like most about your job or studies? ; Would you like to work for a big company? | Bạn đang đi làm hay đi học? Bạn thích điều gì nhất ở công việc hoặc việc học? Bạn có muốn làm cho một công ty lớn không? | I work. I am an accountant in a small company, and I have been there for three years. My colleagues are friendly, although the salary is not high.
P2: Describe a job you would like to do in the future. | what the job is; what skills it needs; why you would be good at it | Kể về một công việc bạn muốn làm trong tương lai: việc gì, cần kỹ năng gì, vì sao bạn hợp với nó, và vì sao bạn muốn làm. | In the future I would like to be the manager of a small department. A manager needs to be a patient leader who can work under pressure and deal with staff fairly. I think I would be good at it because I have worked in the same industry for years and I get on well with my colleagues. My ambition is not fame or a huge income. I simply want a career in which I can make decisions and help younger people.
P3: Is a high salary the most important thing in a job? ; At what age should people retire? | Lương cao có phải là điều quan trọng nhất trong công việc không? Người ta nên nghỉ hưu ở tuổi nào? | I do not think so. A good income matters, but people who are under pressure every day rarely stay in a job for long. My cousin, for example, left a well-paid profession last year, because he had no time for his family, and he says he does not regret it.
#U 30
P1: Do you do any exercise? ; What do you usually have for breakfast? ; Can you cook? | Bạn có tập thể dục không? Bạn thường ăn sáng món gì? Bạn có biết nấu ăn không? | Yes, a little. I walk for thirty minutes every evening, and I exercise at home at the weekend. It is not much, but it keeps me in shape.
P2: Describe a healthy habit that you have. | what the habit is; when you started it; how it helps you | Kể về một thói quen lành mạnh của bạn: thói quen gì, bắt đầu khi nào, nó giúp bạn thế nào, và vì sao bạn giữ nó. | A healthy habit that I have is cooking my own meals. I started two years ago, after I had flu three times in one winter. A doctor told me that my diet was not balanced. Now I buy fresh ingredients at the weekend and cook simple meals with less salt. I have also cut down on sweet drinks. It benefits me a lot: I have more energy, and I have not been ill since then. I keep the habit because it is easy to follow.
P3: Why are many people less healthy today than in the past? ; Should governments limit advertisements for unhealthy food? | Vì sao ngày nay nhiều người kém khỏe hơn trước? Chính phủ có nên hạn chế quảng cáo đồ ăn không lành mạnh không? | I think the main cause is our lifestyle. Many of us sit all day and ignore exercise, and fast food contains too much salt and sugar. My parents, for example, walked or cycled everywhere when they were young, but I go even to the nearest shop by motorbike.
#U 33
P1: What kind of clothes do you like to wear? ; Do you ever visit art galleries? ; Are you good at fixing things? | Bạn thích mặc kiểu quần áo nào? Bạn có bao giờ đi xem triển lãm tranh không? Bạn có giỏi sửa đồ không? | I like simple, practical clothes. I usually wear a cotton shirt and loose trousers, because they suit the hot weather. I do not follow fashion very much.
P2: Describe something that you made yourself. | what it was; what materials you used; how you made it | Kể về một món đồ bạn tự làm: món gì, dùng vật liệu gì, làm thế nào, và bạn cảm thấy ra sao. | Something that I made myself is a small bookshelf. I created it last year, when I could not find a suitable one in the shops. I designed it on paper first, and then I bought some wood and borrowed tools from my uncle. I cut the pieces, made them smooth and fixed them together. The shape is simple, and it is not perfect, but it is practical. Every time I notice it, I feel proud, because I had never made anything before.
P3: Why do fashions change so quickly? ; Is it important to keep old buildings? | Vì sao thời trang thay đổi nhanh như vậy? Giữ lại các tòa nhà cũ có quan trọng không? | I think companies are the main reason. They design new styles every season so that customers feel that their clothes are out of fashion. Tight jeans, for example, were everywhere five years ago, and now most shops near my house sell only loose ones.

P1: What kind of weather do you like? ; Do you like spending time in the countryside? ; Do you recycle at home? | Bạn thích kiểu thời tiết nào? Bạn có thích về vùng quê không? Ở nhà bạn có tái chế không? | I like mild weather, like spring in the north. I do not enjoy the summer heatwaves, and I do not like freezing days either.
P2: Describe a natural place that you have visited. | where it is; what you saw there; what the weather was like | Kể về một nơi thiên nhiên bạn đã đến: ở đâu, bạn thấy gì, thời tiết thế nào, và vì sao bạn nhớ nơi đó. | A natural place that I remember well is Ba Be Lake. The lake is surrounded by forest, and we saw a lot of wildlife, including some amazing birds and insects. The climate was mild, although there was a shower every afternoon. I remember it because the local people preserve the environment so carefully: there was no litter anywhere.

Vì sao một số loài vật có nguy cơ tuyệt chủng? | I believe small actions matter. People can recycle, use fewer plastic bags and stop dropping litter. In my street, for example, a few families started to recycle two years ago, and now almost every house has two bins. If these habits become global, the effect will be huge.

Bạn làm gì khi căng thẳng? | My little nephew makes me laugh. He is quite naughty, and he tells ridiculous jokes that he invents himself. Even when I am in a bad mood, he cheers me up.

Once, in a very serious meeting, the lights went out, and he started to tell a joke in the dark. Everybody laughed, and the stress disappeared. I like him because he changes the feeling in a room. When someone is upset or depressed, he always notices and reacts kindly.

For example, my teacher once fell off her chair in class, laughed first, and the whole class relaxed with her.
#U 42
P1: Do you ask other people for advice? ; Do you complain when a service is bad? ; Are you good at solving problems? | Bạn có hay xin lời khuyên của người khác không? Bạn có phàn nàn khi dịch vụ kém không? Bạn có giỏi giải quyết vấn đề không? | Yes, quite often. When I have a serious problem, I talk to my older sister first. She is sensible, and she never criticises me.
P2: Describe a difficult decision that you made. | what the decision was; who you talked to; what the result was | Kể về một quyết định khó khăn của bạn: quyết định gì, bạn đã hỏi ý kiến ai, kết quả thế nào, và bạn có hài lòng không. | A difficult decision that I made was leaving my first job. The company was good, but I had no spare time and I doubted that I could continue. My parents warned me that it was a risk, and a friend encouraged me to wait. I had a long discussion with my manager, and she refused to change my hours. So I left. The result was positive: I found a job with a sensible schedule, and I have never regretted it.
P3: Is it better to solve problems alone or to ask for help? ; Why do people enjoy gossip? | Tự giải quyết vấn đề hay nhờ người khác giúp thì tốt hơn? Vì sao người ta thích buôn chuyện? | In my view, it is better to ask for help with serious problems. Another person can see the cause more clearly, because he or she is not under pressure. When I could not get rid of a debt, for example, a colleague convinced me to make a simple plan, and it worked.
